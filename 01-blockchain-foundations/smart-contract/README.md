## Nick Szabo

The first person to introduce the concept of smart contracts.

- **Year of introduction:** 1994
- **Famous article:** *Smart Contracts: Building Blocks for Digital Markets*
- **Main idea:** Contracts are implemented as code and can be trusted to execute according to predefined rules.

![Nick Szabo](../../assets/smart-contract/nick-szabo.png)

## Where was the smart contract first implemented in practice?

**Ethereum (2015)**

With the idea and leadership of Vitalik Buterin:

- It was the first platform to implement smart contracts in a practical, programmable way.
- **Programming language:** Solidity
- **Execution environment:** EVM (Ethereum Virtual Machine)

That’s why many people associate smart contracts with Ethereum, but the concept is much older.

![Smart Contract](../../assets/smart-contract/smart-contract-ethereum.png)

## Smart Contract

A smart contract is a program or computer code that is stored and executed on a blockchain. These contracts are designed to execute automatically, without the need for an intermediary, when predetermined conditions are met. In other words, a smart contract is a digital agreement that is executed and managed automatically by code.

## Smart Contract Features

1. **Automatic:** Once deployed on the blockchain, contract execution can occur automatically without human intervention.
2. **Transparency:** The terms and execution of a smart contract may be publicly visible on public blockchains.
3. **Unchangeable:** Deployed contract code and blockchain records are generally difficult to alter; however, whether a contract's behavior can be changed depends on its design.
4. **No Need for Intermediaries:** Smart contracts can reduce reliance on intermediaries and may reduce costs and processing time.

![Smart Contract Features](../../assets/smart-contract/smart-contract-features.png)

## How It Works

1. **Definition of Conditions:** Developers define the contract's rules using a programming language such as Solidity for Ethereum.
2. **Deployment on Blockchain:** The contract is deployed to the blockchain network.
3. **Execution:** When a transaction calls the contract and its programmed conditions are satisfied, the contract executes its code. Some contracts also depend on external data supplied through mechanisms such as oracles.

## Why Are Smart Contracts Important?

Smart contracts change the role of intermediaries. In the traditional world, agreements may involve:

- Banks
- Lawyers
- Notaries
- Companies
- Other third parties

Smart contracts can:

- Reduce some costs.
- Increase execution speed.
- Reduce certain kinds of human error.
- Enable systems to operate around the clock.

## This means that the future of the digital economy can increasingly operate without traditional intermediaries.
